Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 — Publisher: Cymrix

Cymrix ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our web application and tools.

1. Summary — Zero Tracking Architecture

  • No Data Harvesting: We do not collect, track, store, sell, or rent your personal data, artwork, project files, or usage history.
  • Client-Side Operations: The application runs directly inside your web browser. Your artwork, animation frames, layers, custom palettes, and canvas edits remain entirely on your device.
  • No Account Required: You can use the application without registering an account or disclosing personal identification.

2. Local Storage Usage

The application uses standard browser storage mechanisms (localStorage, IndexedDB, and session cache) strictly to store local user preferences and session data, including:

  • Canvas state, layers, and animation frames for autosave / project recovery
  • Saved color palettes, custom brushes, and tool presets
  • Application settings (theme preferences, layout configurations, canvas dimensions)

All local storage data remains on your local machine and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data or cache.

3. Network & External Services

  1. Local Export & File Loading: Files you load into the application (images, palettes) or export (PNGs, GIFs, project files) are processed locally on your device.
  2. Hosting Infrastructure: When accessed over the web, basic server request logs (such as IP address, user agent, and timestamp) may be logged temporarily by hosting infrastructure (e.g. web servers or content delivery networks) strictly for security and network delivery purposes. No artwork or project content is transmitted to external tracking servers.

4. Third-Party Services

This software does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking scripts (e.g., Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel), or data brokers.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for users of all ages. Because we do not collect personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

6. Security

Because your projects and artwork are stored locally in your browser memory, security of your local data relies on the security of your device and web browser. We recommend keeping your web browser updated and backing up important project files regularly.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
